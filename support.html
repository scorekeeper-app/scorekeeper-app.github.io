Support

Tally keeps score for game night, with everyone watching live on their own phone.

Contact

Email scorekeeper@knowledgesystemslabs.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you use and what you were doing when the problem happened.

To include technical details, open Settings > Privacy > Prepare feedback report in the app and attach the report. It holds no player names, scores or join codes.

Sharing a game

  • People in the room: open the game, tap the share button and switch on sharing with people nearby. Others tap Join with a code and type the six-character code. This works without internet.
  • People far away: share the iCloud link from the same screen. Everyone needs to be signed in to iCloud.
  • Guests need no app. The host adds them by name and enters their scores.

iCloud shows "Off"

Check that you are online and signed in to iCloud in the iPhone's Settings app, and that iCloud Drive is on. Tally checks again each time you open it. Nothing is lost meanwhile; scores catch up when iCloud is reachable.

Nearby phones cannot find each other

iOS asks for Local Network access the first time you share or join. If it was declined, open the iPhone's Settings app, go to Privacy & Security > Local Network and turn Tally on. Keep Wi-Fi and Bluetooth switched on.

A score is wrong

The host can tap any score on the sheet to correct it, and every change can be undone from History. If a game has ended, the host can reopen it within 24 hours.

Tally Pro

  • Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It removes the 8-seat limit for games you host and allows unlimited custom games.
  • Restore a purchase: Settings > Tally Pro > Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
