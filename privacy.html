Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026. Contact: scorekeeper@knowledgesystemslabs.com.

The short version

Tally keeps score for your games. We do not run servers for it, we do not have accounts, and we do not collect, see or sell your data. Your games live on your phone. If you share a game, it goes through Apple's iCloud or straight between phones nearby.

What the app stores

  • On your phone: your games, scores, player names, your roster, custom rules you made or imported, and your settings.
  • In your iCloud, if you share a game over iCloud: the game's events, encrypted, in a private area of your iCloud account. People you invite can read (and, if you allow, add to) that game through Apple's sharing. We cannot read it and have no access to your iCloud account or to anyone else's.
  • Between nearby phones: if you share with a join code, phones in the room exchange the game directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, encrypted. Nothing goes through the internet.

What other people can see

Anyone you give a join code or an iCloud link to can see that game, including player names and scores. Share links only with people you want to see them. You can stop sharing at any time from the game's Invite screen; people keep the copy they already have.

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code. If you choose to send us a feedback report, you share it yourself; it contains technical information (game types, counts, error messages, app and iOS version, device model) but no player names, scores or join codes.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to answer you.

Purchases

Tally Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only learns from Apple whether Pro has been bought.

Apple's services

iCloud sharing and App Store purchases are provided by Apple and are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Local Network: to find and connect to phones in the same room.
  • iCloud: to share games with people far away.
  • Notifications (silent): iCloud uses silent notifications to tell the app a shared game changed. No alerts are shown.

Children

The app is for general audiences and does not ask for personal information.

Deleting your data

Delete a game in the app to remove it from your phone. Stop sharing to remove the iCloud copy you created. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone. Data in other people's copies is theirs to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for material changes, say so in the app.
